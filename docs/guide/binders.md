# Binders

[Bibliotaph](../../README.md) › Guide › Binders

A binder is your next session, gathered in one place: the books and pages you'll need, in the order you'll need them,
with your notes. At the table you step through it instead of hunting through folders.

## Make a binder

On the **Binders** page, click **New binder**. Give it a title and, if you like, the date you'll play. Binders with a
date list under **Upcoming**, soonest first; the rest under **No date yet**, and past ones behind a link at the bottom.

The binder you worked on last is your current one. Home shows it under **Continue preparing**.

## Add books and pages

- **A whole book**: **Add to binder** on a book's right-click menu or in its details, or for many books, in the
  Library's **Select** mode.
- **One page**: the **+** beside a matching page in the search results, or **Add page** in the reader. A page added from
  the reader is labelled after the bookmark it's under.
- **A range of pages**: in the reader, the menu beside **Add page** has **Add pages…**. Give the range a label you'll
  recognize at the table, such as "Warehouse ambush", and pick a section.

Each of these adds to your current binder, and lets you pick another binder or make a new one. A book can be in a
binder more than once, at different pages.

## Run a session

Open a binder from the **Binders** page, from Home or from a pin. Its items are listed down the left, the reader is in
the middle, and the binder's notes are on the right.

- **Next** and **Previous** (or **Ctrl+Right** and **Ctrl+Left**) open each item at its first page. The next item in
  the same book shows at once, and the next book is opened in the background so it's ready when you get there.
- The reader's header shows where you are, such as "p. 42–44 · 1 of 3". Wander off into the rest of the book, and
  **Back to this item's pages** brings you back. In the page thumbnails, the item's pages are marked.
- **Add this page** adds the page you're looking at straight after the item in hand, ready to name.
- Rename an item where it is: double-click it, or select it and press **F2**. **Enter** saves, **Esc** cancels.
- The notes on the item's pages show under **On these pages**, and the binder's own notes under **Binder notes**,
  saved as you type.
- **Pop out** moves the binder into its own window, for a second screen. **Return to main window** brings it back at
  the same page.

### Rules at hand

Some binders aren't for one session: a binder of rules, tables and references you look up mid-game. When you make one
(or later, with **Edit title and date**), tick **Keep at hand during games**, and choose its game system if you like.
These binders list under **At hand** on the Binders page.

In any other binder, the **At hand** button lists them with their pages, those for the game you're running first.
Pick one and the page opens in a window of its own, so the binder and page you're on stay put.

## Edit a binder

**Edit binder** in the binder's reader shows the full list:

- **Add section** puts in a heading: **Adventure**, **Maps**, **Encounters**, **Rules**, or **Another name…**.
- Drag an item by its grip to move it, or use **Alt+Up** and **Alt+Down**. Each item's menu moves it to another
  section.
- Each item has a label (blank shows the book's title) and a note.
- **Duplicate** copies the binder (without its date) for a similar session; **Delete** removes it, with **Undo**. The
  books stay in your library either way.
- **Show these books in the Library** shows just this binder's books; **Find resources** opens the Library to find
  more.
- **Done** goes back to the reader.

## When a book changes

Binder items point at pages, and follow them:

- **A new version of a book** (an updated PDF from the publisher): each item is found in the new version by its text.
  An item that can't be found opens at the same printed page, marked "This page changed: check it", with
  **Use this page**. Needs review lists them too.
- **Another copy of the book**: items open at the matching pages in whichever copy opens.
- **A book on a drive that isn't plugged in**, or a file that's gone: the item stays in the binder, saying why, with
  **Try again** or **Find it**.
