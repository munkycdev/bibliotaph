# Collections, favorites and pins

[Bibliotaph](../../README.md) › [Guide](../../README.md#the-guide) › Collections, favorites and pins

None of this moves or copies a file. A book can be in as many collections as you like, and removing it from one only
takes it out of the group.

## Favorites

Mark a book with a heart: hover over its cover and click the heart, choose **Add to favorites** on its right-click
menu, or click the heart in its details or in the reader's toolbar. **Favorites**, pinned in the sidebar from the
start, shows them all, and `favorite:yes` finds them in a search. For an image in a pack, the heart goes on the pack.

## Collections

A collection gathers books around a campaign, a setting or an idea, such as "Winter campaign" or "Coastal towns".

- **Make one**: **New collection** on the **Collections** page. Give it a name and, if you like, a description.
- **Add books**: **Add to collection** on a book's right-click menu or in its details. To add many at once, tick them
  in the Library's **Select** mode and click **Add to collection**.
- **Open one**: click its card. You see its books in the Library, ready to search and filter, with any collections
  inside it as cards above them.
- **Collections inside collections**: **New collection** inside a collection makes one there, and **Move to…** moves a
  collection inside another, or back to the top. A book in an inner collection counts as in the outer one too;
  **Only books added here** hides those.
- **Tidy up**: **Rename**, **Move to…** and **Delete** are at the top of a collection's page. Deleting a collection
  never removes its books from your library. **Remove from collection** (in **Select** mode) takes books out.

Collections, the collections inside each one, and the **Add to collection** menu are in name order. Every change shows
**Undo** for a moment.

### Collections from folders

If you already keep books together in folders, Needs review offers to make those folders collections: "Make a
collection “Ashen Vale” from these 12 books?" for each folder with three or more books directly in it.

- **Accept** makes the collection, named after the folder, with its books. Books that land in the folder later join it
  by themselves, until you add or remove a book by hand; after that it's an ordinary collection. **Undo** takes it away
  again.
- A folder inside another that you made a collection from becomes a collection inside that one.
- **Not now** puts the card away until Needs review makes its list again (when you click **Show the new list**, or the
  next time you start Bibliotaph). **Never** means that folder isn't asked about again.
- Folders named only after what the books are, such as "D&D 5e", "Pathfinder" or "Adventures", aren't offered: those
  are already filters. Neither is a folder whose books are all in one collection already.
- Bibliotaph remembers your answers by the folder's place inside your library folder, so they still hold when a drive
  letter changes. The collections travel to your other PCs with library sync like any other; whether one follows its
  folder is kept on each PC.

### Books for your collections

Needs review also looks for books that belong in a collection you have: "Add these 4 books to “Ashen Vale”?". It
looks at what most of the collection's books share, meaning at least three of them and at least 60% of the books you
added to it, and suggests the books outside it that share it too:

- the same **series**, or
- the same **setting** and **game system** together.

**Accept** adds them, with **Undo**; it doesn't stop a collection made from a folder following that folder. **Not
now** puts the card away until Needs review makes its list again. **Never** means those books aren't suggested for that
collection again, though a book that fits later still can be. Never is kept on each PC.

## Pins

Pin anything you go back to often, and it shows in the sidebar's **Pinned** group and on Home. You can pin a book,
Favorites, a collection, a binder or a saved search: choose **Pin** on a book's menu, or click the pin button in its
details or in the reader, or on the page of a collection, binder, saved search or Favorites.

Drag a pin up or down the sidebar to reorder it, or select it and press **Alt+Up** or **Alt+Down**. Right-click a pin
for **Move up**, **Move down** and **Unpin**, and for a saved search, **Rename…** and **Delete**.

Clicking a pinned book opens it where you left it. A book you own online opens in the online reader; see
[Online libraries](online-libraries.md).

## Saved searches

A saved search keeps a search with its filters and order, and runs it again each time, so new books show up in it.
See [Saved searches](searching.md#saved-searches).

## Export a list

In **Select** mode, **Export these** saves the ticked books as a CSV file for a spreadsheet. **Settings > Backup** can
export the whole library the same way; see [OneDrive, drives and other PCs](onedrive-and-other-pcs.md#backups).
