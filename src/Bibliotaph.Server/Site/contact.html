Bibliotaph is made by one person, who reads everything that comes in.

Problems and ideas

The quickest way to report a problem or suggest something is an issue on GitHub. Say what you did, what you expected and what happened instead. Please don't include book titles or text you'd rather keep private: issues are public.

In the app, Settings › About Bibliotaph has the version you're using, which helps.

Email

For anything else, or anything private, such as your account, your data or an invitation, email munkycdev@bibliotaph.online. We answer within a few days, and requests about your data within 30 days.

Security

If you find a security problem, please email it rather than opening a public issue, so it can be fixed before it's known.
