FOR GAME MASTERS

Find the right book, and the right page, in the RPG library you already have.

Bibliotaph catalogs the PDFs, e-books, maps and handouts in your own folders and cloud storage, right where they are. Search every page of every book, filter by game system, type and level, and open a book at the page you need.

Sign in Get the Windows app

The web version is invite-only for now. The Windows app is free, and needs no account.

Your files stay put

Bibliotaph never moves, renames, edits or deletes your books. It only reads them, in your folders, OneDrive, Google Drive, Dropbox, Box or a WebDAV server.

Search inside every book

Words, phrases and fields such as type:adventure level:3, across the text of every page, scanned pages included. A result opens at its page.

A catalog that fills itself in

Game system, type, levels, players and setting, from the file's name and, if you like, an AI that quotes the page it found each one on. You check the exceptions, not every book.

Ready for the table

Collections for a campaign, binders with the pages for a session and your notes, and a reader that steps through them.

Online libraries too

Books you own on D&D Beyond, Demiplane, Humble Bundle or DriveThruRPG join the library beside your files, with a link to where you read or download them.

On your PC and on the web

The Windows app works offline with nothing sent anywhere. Connect it to the web version, and your work and what the AI read follow you to every device.
