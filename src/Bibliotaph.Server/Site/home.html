HOW IT WORKS

Ready in three steps.

  1. 1

    Point it at your books

    Choose your folders, drives or cloud storage. Nothing is copied, moved or renamed.

  2. 2

    Let it read

    Bibliotaph reads every page, recognises scans, joins duplicate copies and fills in the catalog.

  3. 3

    Find anything

    Search, filter and open books at the right page, and build binders for the table.

QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Does Bibliotaph change my files?

Never. It only reads them, wherever they are. Your corrections, notes, collections and binders live in Bibliotaph's own catalog, which you can back up and export.

Which games does it work with?

Any. D&D, Pathfinder, Call of Cthulhu, indie zines, maps and handouts: if it's a PDF, an EPUB, a Kindle book or a picture, Bibliotaph reads it, ZIPs included.

Do I need AI?

No. Search, filters, binders and the catalog all work without it. AI fills in details faster, and on Windows it can run entirely on your own computer.

What does it cost?

The Windows app is free and open source. The web version is free while it's open by invitation.

Can it open protected PDFs?

A PDF with a password asks for it, and Bibliotaph respects a book's copy restrictions. It never guesses passwords or removes DRM.

Will my work follow me between computers?

Yes. Connect the Windows app to your web library, and your corrections, notes, collections and binders merge both ways, on every PC and browser you use.
